01 The find

Cheap to fix. Expensive to build.

An order-cancellation process was already being mapped, on its way to automation. My manager came to me with a question about it. A question, not a task.

To answer it properly I read the whole flow, not only my part of it. The draft still held inconsistencies and gaps in the logic, so I flagged them, joined the analysis and helped shape the version that went to implementation, while it was all still on paper.

I am not going to claim that automation as mine. It would most likely have been built without me. What I changed is that it was built on a process that held up, and it got there faster. An error caught during design costs a conversation. The same error caught after launch costs a rebuild.
